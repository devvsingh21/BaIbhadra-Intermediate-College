About the school

A student-first intermediate college rooted in academic progress and character building.

BaIbhadra Intermediate College serves students and families around Deeha with a focus on disciplined learning, teacher guidance, co-curricular participation, and transparent communication with parents.

Mission

To help every learner build strong fundamentals, responsible habits, confidence, and respect for society.

Vision

To become a trusted local institution known for learning outcomes, values, safety, and opportunity.

Founder

Founded by Balbhadra Bahadur Singh in 1965 with a commitment to accessible education for the local community.
